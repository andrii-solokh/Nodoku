Music behind the connections

Every connection plays the next note of a melody. Discover the composer, then open the score to explore the full piece.

These are short, synthesized, single-line adaptations, not recordings. Accompaniment is omitted; some chord tones and repeats are omitted. The score’s note spacing and rests guide automatic playback, while your moves set the pace during play.
